Pattern

Notifications

Toasts answer what you just did. Banners describe the state of an event until it changes. The notification centre keeps what happened while you were away.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Built from toast, alert (page banner), list and badge (unread count). The mocks have the save toast (with action and stacked), the RSVP toast, the event status banner, the guest import alerts, the invitation page banner and the Notifications page.

Problems found in a stepInline alert in that stepWhile the step is open“3 guests share an email address”

Structure

The Notifications page: unread items carry an accent dot and “unread” in text, each title links to what it reports, and Mark read sits at the end of the row.

  1. Priority danger before warning before info; one banner per event at a time, the most urgent.
  2. Grouping three toasts at most; older ones leave first. Bounces for one event are one item, not one per email.
  3. Dismissal toasts time out except errors; banners that need action cannot be dismissed, only resolved.
  4. Record anything that matters after the toast leaves is also in the notification centre.

States

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts: bottom-right, 24rem wide from 36rem; full width at the bottom below that, above the safe area. Newest at the bottom.
  • Banners span the content column under the event tabs; their actions wrap under the text on phones.
  • The notification centre is a list at every width; Mark read stays on the row and wraps under the text at 360px.
  • Toasts never cover the focused element or the primary action of a sheet; on phones they appear above a dialog's sticky footer.

Accessibility

  • Two live regions per page: role="status" (polite) for toasts and saved states, role="alert" (assertive) for errors (L2-087). Never nest one in the other.
  • Toasts and banners enter over --duration-base; under reduced motion they appear without sliding or fading (L2-089).
  • Banners that appear on page load are plain content (role="status" at most); only a banner that appears after an action uses role="alert".
  • Toasts last at least 8 seconds, pause on hover and focus, and have a labelled Dismiss button; error toasts have no timer (SC 2.2.1).
  • Unread is said in text (“· unread”), not only with the accent dot (SC 1.4.1); the count is in the link's accessible name.
  • Each Mark read button names its item.
  • Toast actions (Undo, Try again, Resend) are reachable with the keyboard while the toast is visible; focus is not moved to them.

Content

  • Titles are short facts without a full stop: “Invites are open”, “2 invitations failed”, “RSVP saved”. The body is a sentence, with one.
  • Say what happens next or what to do: “3 bounced. We’ll show them under Emails.”, “Fix the email addresses in Guests, then resend.”
  • Name the event in the notification centre (“for Maya’s 30th birthday”) because items mix events.
  • Times in the event time zone; copy must agree with the settings: “Times use the event time zone.”
  • Guest-facing banners speak to the guest about their reply: “You can change your answer until Sat 7 Nov.”
