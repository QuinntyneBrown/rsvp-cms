List

A list stacks records that are read one at a time: the latest replies, notifications, upcoming events, the evening’s schedule. Each row has one title, an optional line of meta and at most one trailing element.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <ul> / <ol>

Overview

Use a list when each record stands alone and people scan titles rather than compare columns. Use a table when they compare values across records (reply, party size, meal), and a description list for labelled facts about one thing.

  • Two-line: title plus meta; the event overview’s “Latest replies”.
  • Interactive: the whole row is one link; the dashboard’s “Upcoming events”.
  • Notification: unread dot, title, meta that says “unread”, and one row action.
  • Schedule: an ordered list with a fixed time column; the public event page.
  • With avatar: initials before the title for people lists.

Anatomy

  1. Leading element (optional)Unread .dot--accent, an avatar or a .list__time.
  2. Title.list__title, --text-label; semibold when unread.
  3. Meta.list__meta, --text-body-sm in --color-fg-muted; one line, joined with “·”.
  4. Trailing element (optional)One badge or one small ghost button.
  5. Row.list__item: 12 × 16px padding, 12px gap, 1px divider except after the last row.

Responsive behaviour

  • Lists are already one column; at 360px the meta line wraps and the trailing badge or button stays vertically centred.
  • Titles may wrap to two lines; use .truncate on the meta only when it is a URL or email.
  • The schedule’s time column keeps a 4.5rem minimum so descriptions align even when they wrap.
  • Interactive rows are at least 44px tall (12px padding around two lines of text), so they are comfortable touch targets.

Theming

Lists read --color-border-default for dividers, --color-bg-subtle for hover, --color-bg-subtle-hover for active and --color-accent-subtle for the selected row. The unread dot is --color-accent. Lists have no background of their own; they take the card or page they sit on.

In dark the dividers use --palette-gray-775, just above the gray-900 surface, so rows separate without bright lines.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <ul> or <ol> (the schedule is ordered). Screen readers announce “list, 3 items”. An interactive row is one <a> (navigation) or <button> (action) inside the <li>, never a clickable <li>. No ARIA listbox: the list is not a selection widget.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between interactive rows and row actions.
EnterFollows a row link or activates a row action.

Focus

Interactive rows show the shared ring inset by its own width so it is not clipped by the card’s rounded, overflow-hidden frame.

Labelling

The row’s text is its accessible name, so keep the title first. Row actions repeat the object (“Mark read: 2 invitation emails bounced”). The unread dot is aria-hidden; “unread” is in the meta text.

Motion

Row hover fades over --duration-fast; instant with reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive rows are at least 44px tall, and under (pointer: coarse) trailing small buttons such as “Mark read” grow to 44px (L2-084).

Content

  • Titles say what happened or what the thing is: “Jonah Adeyemi replied yes to Maya’s 30th birthday”.
  • Meta joins short facts with “ · ”: “Attending · 2 guests · 2 min ago”.
  • Times in the schedule use the 12-hour clock with lowercase am/pm: “6:30 pm”.
  • Quote guest messages in curly quotes: “Sorry, I’m in Lisbon”.
