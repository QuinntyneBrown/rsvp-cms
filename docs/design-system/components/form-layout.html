Form layout

Form layout arranges fields into a form people can finish: one column, related fields side by side, a summary of what to fix, and one clear place for the submit button.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<form novalidate>

Overview

Every Invitely form is built from form fields stacked with .stack, grouped with <fieldset>, and ended by an actions bar. The rules for validation timing and messages are in the forms pattern; this page covers the pieces.

  • Single column (default): the RSVP form, sign-in, account, new event.
  • Two columns (.form-row): short related fields side by side from 48rem, such as date and start time.
  • Inline: one field and its button on one row, such as guest search.
  • Actions bar (.form-actions): end-aligned by default; --between when the secondary action is a different path (RSVP “Can’t make it?”); --start when it follows left-aligned content.
  • Error summary (.form-summary): a danger alert at the top listing each problem as a link to its field.

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryDanger alert, role="alert", tabindex="-1"; receives focus after a failed submit.
  2. FieldLabel, input, help and error, --space-1 apart.
  3. Row.form-row: two columns from 48rem with --space-4 between.
  4. Second columnOnly for a short field that belongs with its neighbour.
  5. Actions barPrimary last; --space-4 above, --space-3 between buttons.

Sizes

Forms have no sizes. Width comes from the container: cards in .container--narrow (42rem) for public and auth forms, the page container for organiser forms.

States

A form is in one of four states. The fields keep their own states (see text field).

StateWhat changes
DefaultFields editable; submit enabled. Nothing is validated until the first submit or blur.
InvalidError summary at the top with a link per error, receives focus; each bad field gets aria-invalid and a .field__error; the text in the summary and under the field is identical.
SubmittingThe submit button sets aria-busy="true" and keeps its label; other fields are left alone (not readonly, not disabled); Cancel still works.
SuccessEither the next page, or a success alert (role="status") in place of the form with the next step as a button.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column below 48rem. .form-row becomes two equal columns from 48rem; never three.
  • Public and auth forms sit in .container--narrow (42rem) so lines stay readable; organiser forms use the full container but fields never exceed the 65ch measure.
  • The actions bar wraps: at 360px the RSVP buttons stack with “Send my RSVP” on its own row. The primary stays last in DOM order.
  • Inside a dialog the actions bar is the sticky .dialog__footer, so it stays visible while the body scrolls.

Theming

Form layout has no colours of its own: spacing comes from --space-1/2/3/4, the summary from the danger alert tokens (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-danger-border) and a --space-1 left edge.

In dark the summary becomes a deep red-950 panel with red-300 text and links; its links inherit the text colour so they never show as blue on red.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form> with novalidate (browser bubbles are replaced by the system's messages) and an accessible name (aria-label or a heading via aria-labelledby). Groups use <fieldset>/<legend>. Search forms carry role="search".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through fields in visual order, then the actions; the primary is last.
EnterIn a text field, submits the form.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field (the link targets its id).

Focus

After a failed submit, focus moves to the error summary (it has tabindex="-1") so the count is announced. After success, focus moves to the success message or the new page's <h1>.

Labelling

Every field has a visible label; required is the default and “(optional)” is written out. Field errors are linked with aria-describedby; group errors are linked from the <fieldset>. The summary title counts the problems (“2 things to fix”).

Motion

None of its own. The summary appears without animation so focus and announcement are immediate.

Touch

Fields are 40px tall and 16px apart; actions are 40px buttons with 12px between them; at 360px the primary is full width when it wraps.

Content

  • Summary title counts: “1 thing to fix”, “2 things to fix”, “3 rows need attention”.
  • Each summary item is the field's own error message, word for word, linking to the field.
  • Order fields as people think: what, when, where (event name, date, start time, venue).
  • Primary button names the outcome: “Create event”, “Send my RSVP”, “Save changes”.
  • Mark the exceptions: “(optional)” on optional fields; never an asterisk legend.
