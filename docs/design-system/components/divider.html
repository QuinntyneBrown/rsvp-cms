Divider

A divider is a 1px rule that separates groups inside one surface. Most rules in Invitely belong to a component (card footer, dialog footer, list rows); a standalone divider is for the few places those do not cover.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<hr>

Overview

Use a divider between two groups inside one card or panel that are related but distinct, such as the email and password rows on the account page. Do not add one where spacing already separates the groups, between cards (the gap does that), above a card or dialog footer (the footer draws its own rule), or between list items (.list__item draws them).

  • Horizontal: <hr>, 24px above and below.
  • Vertical: between inline groups in a toolbar row; at least 24px tall.
  • With label: a caption centred between two rules, to name what follows in a long list.

Anatomy

  1. Rule1px --color-border-default; decorative.
  2. Label (optional)--text-caption, --color-fg-muted, 12px from each rule.

Sizes

One weight: a 1px hairline (--border-width-hairline). Horizontal dividers take 24px (--space-6) above and below; vertical dividers stretch to the row height, at least 24px.

States

Dividers are static: no interaction states.

  • In dark the rule is gray-775 on the gray-900 surface: visible, but quieter than a control border.

Responsive behaviour

  • Horizontal dividers span their container at every width.
  • A vertical divider in a .cluster disappears from view when the row wraps; if the groups then stack, the divider has no job, so hide it below 36rem or switch to horizontal.
  • Labelled dividers wrap their label onto two lines rather than shrinking the rules below 24px.

Theming

Reads --color-border-default and --border-width-hairline; the label reads --color-fg-muted and --text-caption. Under prefers-contrast: more the default border becomes the strong border automatically, so dividers darken with no extra rule.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<hr> has the implicit separator role. A vertical divider is a <span role="separator" aria-orientation="vertical">. A divider that is purely visual inside a group can be aria-hidden="true"; a labelled divider that names a group should be a heading instead if people need to navigate to it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; separators are not interactive.

Focus

None.

Labelling

The label text in .divider--label is read in order; keep it meaningful on its own.

Contrast

The rule is decorative and exempt from 1.4.11: the groups are also separated by space. The label is text and must meet 4.5:1.

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a target. Keep 8px or more between a vertical divider and the controls beside it.

Content

  • A divider label is a short noun phrase in sentence case, no full stop: “Replies after the invite window closed”.
  • If the label would be a heading, use a heading.
