Pagination

Pagination splits long lists into pages of 20: the guest list, the responses table and the events list. It says where you are (“Showing 1–20 of 84”) and lets you move one page or jump to any.

Overview

The three “long” mocks (events, guests, event responses) end with a pagination bar. Lists of 20 or fewer rows have none.

  • Use pagination for tables an organiser scans and acts on (select guests, export responses), where a stable page and URL matter.
  • Don't paginate the notifications list or a card's “Latest replies”; show the newest few and link to the full page.
  • Page size is 20. Filters and search reset to page 1.

Anatomy

  1. Status“Showing 1–20 of 84”, caption type, tabular figures.
  2. PreviousIcon-like “‹” button named “Previous page”; disabled on page 1.
  3. Current pageAccent-subtle fill and accent border, aria-current="page".
  4. Gap“…” when pages are skipped; hidden from screen readers.
  5. Next“›” button named “Next page”; disabled on the last page.

Sizes

One size: 32px items (--control-height-sm), growing to 44px under coarse pointers (L2-084).

States

Page buttons have default, hover, focus, current and disabled. Previous is disabled on the first page and Next on the last, using the native disabled attribute.

Responsive behaviour

  • Status and controls share a row (.pagination-bar, space-between) and wrap onto two rows when they do not fit: status above, controls below.
  • At 360px with a mouse the numbered variant (at most 7 items plus previous and next, 9 × 32px + gaps ≈ 320px) fits on one row; with 44px touch targets it wraps to two.
  • Inside cards narrower than 20rem use the simple variant.

Theming

Items use --color-fg-muted on transparent, --color-bg-subtle on hover, and the accent-subtle fill with --color-accent border for the current page, matching selected chips and the current sidebar link.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav aria-label="Pagination"> landmark containing a list of buttons (in-page reload of the table) or links (when each page has its own URL, e.g. ?page=2). The current page has aria-current="page".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through previous, the page numbers and next; disabled buttons are skipped.
Enter / SpaceGoes to that page.

Focus

After a page change, focus moves to the table's caption or first row heading so keyboard users start at the top of the new page, and the status text updates.

Labelling

Numbers are named “Page 3”; previous and next are named in words because “‹” and “›” are not announced usefully. The “…” gap is aria-hidden. The status line is plain text, read when the nav is reached.

Motion

None; the table changes in place.

Touch

Items are 32px with 4px between them for mouse users. Under (pointer: coarse) they grow to 44 × 44px (--target-comfortable, L2-084); the numbered variant then wraps onto a second row at 360px rather than shrinking.

Content

  • Status: “Showing 1–20 of 84” with an en dash and no unit; the table caption already says what is listed.
  • Simple variant: “Page 2 of 5”.
  • Accessible names: “Previous page”, “Next page”, “Page 3”.
