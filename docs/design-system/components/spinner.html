Spinner

A spinner says “working on it” for a wait of a second or two whose length you cannot measure. Busy buttons draw their own spinner; this component is for everywhere else.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="status"

Overview

Use a spinner for a short wait in a small area: refreshing one card, checking an invitation code, loading the next page of a list. Use a skeleton when a page or region loads for the first time (the layout is known), a progress bar when you can measure the work, and a busy button when the wait follows a button press.

  • Inline with text: “Checking your code…” next to the invitation-code field.
  • Standalone: centred in a card or region that is refreshing.
  • No mock uses a standalone spinner today; every wait in the mocks is a busy button or a skeleton. The component exists so the next wait does not invent its own.

Anatomy

  1. Ring2px border in --color-border-default with a --color-accent arc; rotates every --duration-spin.
  2. Text (optional)What is happening; otherwise visually hidden text gives the status its name.

Both themes

LoadingChecking your code…
  • A spinner has no interactive states.
  • Show it only after about 300 ms, so instant responses never flash; once shown, keep it at least 500 ms so it does not flicker.
  • Under reduced motion it keeps turning at a third of the speed: it is a status indicator, so stopping it would read as “stuck”.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes do not change with the viewport.
  • Inline spinners wrap with their text; standalone spinners stay centred in their region.

Theming

The arc reads --color-accent and the ring --color-border-default; the size is the component token --spinner-size. On an inverse surface (a toast) set the arc to --color-fg-inverse.

.toast .spinner { border-top-color: var(--color-fg-inverse); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The spinner itself is aria-hidden; its wrapper is role="status" with text, visible or visually hidden, so the wait is announced once (WCAG 4.1.3). The region being refreshed also gets aria-busy="true".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNot focusable.

Focus

Never moves focus; if the control that started the wait is replaced, focus moves to its replacement.

Labelling

Name the work, not the widget: “Refreshing replies”, never “Spinner” or “Loading…” alone when you know what is loading.

Motion

Rotates linearly every --duration-spin (800 ms); three times slower under reduced motion.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Present continuous, with the object: “Checking your code…”, “Loading more responses…”.
  • Use the ellipsis character (…), not three dots.
