InlineStatus

A small status line next to actions: “Saved at 10:42 am”, “Draft · saved 2 min ago”, “Saving…”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableReact: InlineStatus

Overview

Confirms autosave and immediate settings without a toast. Lives in a polite live region so changes are announced.

Anatomy

  1. Icon or dot16px, decorative.
  2. TextBody small, tone colour.

Variants

Draft · saved 2 min ago Saved at 10:42 am Not savedSaving…
Neutral, success, danger, busy.

Sizes

One size.
