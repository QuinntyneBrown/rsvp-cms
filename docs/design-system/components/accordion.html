Accordion

An accordion lets guests scan the questions on an invitation page and open only the answers they need. It is built on native <details>, so it works without JavaScript.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <details>

Overview

Use an accordion for a list of independent, optional answers that most readers will skip: the public page's “Questions” block. Do not use one to hide content everyone needs (event time, place, the RSVP form), for steps in a flow (use a stepper), or for a single show-more toggle on an admin page (use a ghost button with aria-expanded, as the CMS blocks' “Collapse” does).

  • Each item is one <details>; several can be open at once (multi-open). There is no single-open variant: closing one answer when another opens loses the reader's place.
  • The whole summary row is the target, at least 44px tall.
  • The answer is plain text or a short paragraph; no forms inside an accordion.

Anatomy

  1. Summary (question)<summary>, --text-label, 12×16px padding, min 44px tall.
  2. Indicator“+” that rotates 45° to “×” when open; decorative, --color-fg-subtle.
  3. Answer.accordion__body, muted body text, 16px side padding.
  4. ContainerHairline border, --radius-md, a rule between items.

Variants

One variant: a bordered, multi-open list. It can stand alone or sit inside a card under a heading, which is how the public page uses it.

Sizes

Accordions have one size; they take the width of their container and the summary row is always at least 44px (--target-comfortable).

States

States belong to the summary row. Hover and focus are forced here with data-state; the open state is the native open attribute.

  • Hover fills the summary row with --color-bg-subtle.
  • Focus draws the shared ring inset, inside the row, so the container border does not clip it.
  • Expanded rotates the indicator and shows the answer; there is no disabled state.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width at every breakpoint; questions wrap onto more lines rather than truncate.
  • At 360px the 16px side padding leaves about 260px for the question, enough for questions of ten words.
  • The indicator stays on the right of the first line of a wrapped question.

Theming

The accordion reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-bg-subtle (hover), --color-fg-muted (answer) and --color-fg-subtle (indicator). It has no component tokens; a theme changes it through the semantic tokens. In dark the container is the gray-900 surface with a gray-775 rule, and the hover fill is gray-800.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <details>/<summary>: browsers expose the summary as a button-like control with its expanded state, which satisfies the APG Accordion intent without ARIA. Put the accordion after a heading (“Questions”) so it is reachable by heading navigation.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between questions.
Enter, SpaceOpens or closes the focused question.
Find in pageChromium opens a closed <details> whose answer matches the search.

Focus

Focus stays on the summary when it toggles. The ring is drawn 2px inside the row so it is not cut off by the container's border radius.

Labelling

The summary text is the name: write it as the question the guest would ask. The “+” indicator is generated content and is not announced.

Motion

Only the indicator rotates, over --duration-base; the answer appears without animation. Reduced motion removes the rotation.

Touch

The whole summary row is the target and is at least 44px tall.

Content

  • Questions in the guest's words, ending with a question mark: “Can I bring a guest?”, not “Plus-one policy”.
  • Answers in one or two sentences, starting with the answer: “Step-free entry on Harbour Street…”.
  • Five to eight questions at most. Anything everyone needs (time, place, dress code) goes in the Details block, not in the FAQ.
