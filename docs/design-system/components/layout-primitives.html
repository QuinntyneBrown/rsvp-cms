Layout primitives

Six small classes lay out every Invitely screen: a container caps the width, a stack spaces things vertically, a cluster lines them up horizontally, grids and the split arrange regions, and two shells frame the organiser app and the sign-in pages.

Overview

Use these primitives instead of margins on individual elements. Spacing belongs to the parent: a .stack spaces its children, so moving or removing a child never leaves a gap or a collision. Columns, gutters and margins come from --layout-columns, --layout-gutter and --layout-margin, which change at 48rem and 62rem (see Layout).

Anatomy

  1. Margin--layout-margin: 16px, 32px from 48rem, 48px from 62rem.
  2. Column--layout-columns: 4, 8 from 48rem, 12 from 62rem. Resize the window: this demo is live.
  3. Gutter--layout-gutter: 16px, 24px from 48rem, 32px from 62rem.

Responsive behaviour

  • Mobile first. Below 36rem: 4 columns, 16px gutters and margins; the sidebar opens as a full-width panel from the top bar's menu button; guest pages are one column with a full-width form.
  • 36rem: the sidebar opens as a 20rem drawer (--layout-drawer-width) from the left.
  • 48rem: 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px margins; .sidebar-layout becomes two columns and the sidebar is persistent.
  • 62rem: 12 columns, 32px gutters, 48px margins; .split shows the aside; .container--guest widens to 60rem so Details and Schedule sit side by side.
  • 80rem and up: the container stops at 80rem and centres.
  • .cluster always wraps; nothing in a cluster may rely on staying on one line.
  • No primitive uses a max-width media query, and none uses viewport-width units for spacing.

Theming

Primitives carry no colour; they read only layout and space tokens, so themes do not affect them. Products with a different grid change --layout-* tokens in tokens.css; one-off widths use the component tokens --container-narrow, --split-aside and --auth-card-max.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Primitives are <div>s with no role. Landmarks come from the elements placed in them: <header class="topbar">, <nav class="sidebar" aria-label="Primary">, <main id="main" class="container">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable. Reading and focus order follow the DOM; never reorder visually with order or grid placement.

Focus

.split puts the editor before the preview in the DOM, so Tab reaches the fields first, matching the visual order.

Labelling

Name the landmarks that the shells create: the sidebar nav is “Primary”; the skip link targets #main.

Contrast

Not applicable: primitives draw nothing.

Motion

None.

Touch

Reflow (WCAG 1.4.10): every shell and primitive works at 320px wide without horizontal scrolling; only tables scroll inside their own wrapper.

Content

Layout primitives contain no copy of their own.
