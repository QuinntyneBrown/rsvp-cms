Page header

The page header says where the organiser is and what they can do here: the page title, one line of context, and the page's actions with the primary one last.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableMobile-first actions

Overview

Every organiser page in the mocks (117) opens with a page header after the breadcrumb. On event pages the title is the event name and the subtitle its date, time and venue; event tabs follow it.

  • Put the page's own actions here: “Add guest”, “Schedule invitations”, “New event”. Row and card actions stay with their row or card.
  • At most one primary action, last. Up to two more as secondary or ghost; anything beyond that goes in a menu.
  • Leave the actions container out entirely when a page has none, rather than leaving an empty one.

Anatomy

  1. TitleThe page's <h1>, --text-h1 with tight tracking; wraps, never truncates.
  2. SubtitleOne line of context in --color-fg-muted, 4px below the title.
  3. Secondary actionsSecondary or ghost buttons/links in .page-header__actions, 12px apart.
  4. Primary actionThe one primary, last in DOM and visual order; full width below 48rem.

Responsive behaviour

  • Base (phones): title and subtitle first; actions below on their own row. The primary action takes the whole last row (flex: 1 1 100%); secondary actions share the row above it in DOM order.
  • 48rem (--layout-breakpoint-md) and up: actions sit to the right of the title, aligned to its baseline (align-items: flex-end), and the primary returns to its natural width.
  • Long event names wrap (text-wrap: balance); the actions wrap below them rather than squeezing the title.
  • Vertical rhythm: 24px above and below (--space-6).

Theming

No colours of its own: the title uses --color-fg-default, the subtitle --color-fg-muted, actions their button tokens. Theming the page header means theming buttons and type.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain structure: the page's single <h1>, a paragraph, then links and buttons. No landmark of its own; it sits at the top of <main>. Actions that navigate (“View public page”, “Import CSV”) are links; actions that open a dialog in the product (“Add guest”) are buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the actions in DOM order: secondary first, primary last.
Enter / SpaceActivates the action (Space on buttons only).

Focus

After a dialog opened from a header action closes, focus returns to that action.

Labelling

The <h1> matches the page <title>'s first part (“Guests · Maya’s 30th birthday · Invitely”). Action labels are verb phrases; icons are aria-hidden.

Motion

None beyond the buttons' hover transitions.

Touch

Actions are 40px buttons, and on phones the primary is the full content width, the easiest target on the page.

Content

  • Title: the thing, not the app (“Maya’s 30th birthday”, “Events”, “Import guests”); no “Manage” or “Welcome to”.
  • Subtitle: one line, facts separated by middle dots (“Sat 14 Nov 2026 · 7:00 pm · The Lantern Room”), or a short count (“3 events · 1 taking invites”).
  • Actions: verb + object, sentence case: “Add guest”, “Schedule invitations”, “New event”.
