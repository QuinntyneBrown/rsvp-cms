PageHeader

Title, one-line subtitle and the page’s actions, under the breadcrumb and above the tabs.

Overview

Every organiser page starts with a PageHeader. The title is the page’s only <h1>. Actions: at most one primary, placed last.

Anatomy

  1. Title<h1>, --text-h1.
  2. SubtitleMuted, one line of context (date, counts).
  3. ActionsCluster of Buttons; primary last.
