Card

A card is the surface that holds one topic: an event's invite window, a CMS block, the danger zone, a block type to pick. Cards sit on the canvas, never inside each other.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.card

Overview

Use a card to group content that belongs together and can be read on its own: a summary on the event overview, one block of the CMS editor, a section of the public invitation page. Use a list inside a flush card for records, a dialog when the content needs the person's full attention, and a well (sunken surface) for content nested inside a card, such as the live preview.

  • Default holds a title and any content; use .card.stack so children are spaced by the card, not by inline margins.
  • Header and footer add a title row with actions and a ruled footer, as in each CMS block.
  • Flush removes padding so a list or table runs edge to edge (dashboard “Upcoming events”, notifications).
  • Selectable is a <button class="card card--interactive"> with aria-pressed, used by the block picker.
  • Danger outlines a group of destructive actions (event settings “Danger zone”).
  • Centred is the public invitation hero: centred text and roomier padding.
  • Well is not a card but its nested companion: sunken, no border or shadow.

Anatomy

  1. Header.card__header: title (--text-h4) with an optional caption, actions on the right; wraps on narrow cards.
  2. Header actionsSmall ghost buttons in a .cluster; icon labels name the block (“Move hero down”).
  3. BodyAny content; 16px below the header. A .stack spaces the fields.
  4. Footer.card__footer: hairline rule, 16px above, actions right-aligned.

Variants

Variants change the card's role, not its look for decoration. All share the surface, hairline border, --radius-lg corners and --shadow-1.

Sizes

Cards have one size: they take the width of their grid cell or container and use 24px padding (--space-6). Flush cards drop the padding; the centred hero uses 32px vertical padding.

States

Static cards have no states. Selectable cards are buttons and have the full set; forced with data-state here, real pages use :hover, :focus-visible, :active, aria-pressed and disabled.

Hero
A well sits inside a card on the sunken surface.
  • Hover lifts the card 1px, strengthens the border and raises the shadow to --shadow-2.
  • Selected keeps the accent border while hovered, so the choice never looks lost.
  • Disabled (the block picker while “Add block” is busy) is 50% opaque and does not lift.
  • In dark the card surface (gray-900) is lighter than the canvas (gray-950) and the well (gray-925) is darker than the card, so depth still reads without shadows.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their cell. Card grids use .grid--auto (18rem minimum), so the overview cards stack to one column on phones and flow to three at 1280px.
  • Padding stays 24px at every width; at 360px that leaves 280px of content inside a full-width card.
  • The header wraps: the title takes the first row and the action cluster drops below it when they do not fit (CMS blocks at 360px).
  • Footer actions wrap and stay right-aligned; a lone destructive action stays at the end.
  • Selectable card grids keep each card at least 44px tall, so they are comfortable touch targets.

Theming

The card reads three component tokens: --card-bg (--color-bg-surface), --card-border (--color-border-default) and --card-padding (--space-6). Variants only reassign them: danger sets --card-border to --color-danger-border; selected sets both to the accent; flush and centred change the padding.

/* A quieter card for a partner theme */
[data-theme="partner"] .card { --card-border: transparent; }

The decorative border is exempt from 1.4.11; meaning never rests on it alone. The danger card carries its meaning in the “Danger zone” heading, and the selected card in aria-pressed.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is presentation. Give it a landmark only when it is a real section: <section aria-labelledby> pointing at the card title, as every card on the event pages does. A selectable card is a native <button aria-pressed> (APG Button), grouped in an element with role="group" and aria-label. A card that navigates contains one real link; never wrap other controls inside a clickable card.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the selectable card, or to the controls inside a static card, in DOM order.
Enter, SpaceToggles a selectable card's aria-pressed.
—Static cards are not focusable.

Focus

Selectable cards show the shared 2px ring outside the card's radius. The ring sits on the dialog surface in the block picker, where it measures 3:1 or more in both themes.

Labelling

The button's name is its content: the title and caption (“Schedule, A timed list of the evening”). Keep captions short so the name stays readable. Section cards are named by their .card__title through aria-labelledby.

Motion

Hover lift and shadow transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion removes the transition through the tokens, and the hover look still applies instantly.

Touch

There is no hover on touch, so selection is shown by the accent surface, not by the lift. Each selectable card is at least 44px tall and the grid gap keeps 24px or more between them.

Content

  • Card titles are nouns in sentence case: “Invite window”, “Public page”, “Latest replies”, “Danger zone”.
  • One idea per card. If a card needs two headings, it is two cards.
  • Selectable card captions describe what the block contains in under eight words: “Headline, supporting line and button”.
  • Danger-zone rows name the action and its consequence: “Delete event — Removes the page, invitees and responses.”
