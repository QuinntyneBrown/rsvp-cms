Tooltip

A tooltip is a small dark label that appears next to an icon-only control when it is hovered or focused. It repeats or extends the control's name; it never holds anything people must read or click.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Tooltip

Overview

Use a tooltip on every icon-only button except a dialog's Close: the top bar's notifications and menu buttons, table row actions, the CMS block arrows. Do not use one for information the person needs to complete a task (use field help), for anything interactive (use a menu or a dialog), or on touch-only flows, where it never appears.

  • Text: one to four words — the control's name or extra state (“3 unread”).
  • With shortcut: the name plus a <kbd> hint.
  • Placement: above the trigger by default; below (.has-tooltip--below) for triggers near the top of the viewport, such as everything in the sticky top bar.

Anatomy

  1. Label--text-caption, no wrapping, --color-fg-inverse.
  2. Container and arrow--color-bg-inverse, --radius-sm, 4×8px padding, --shadow-2, a 5px arrow toward the trigger.
  3. TriggerThe icon-only control; the tooltip is its child, so hover and focus-within reveal it.

Sizes

Tooltips have one size: caption text with 4×8px padding. They grow in width with the label and never wrap.

States

The tooltip has two states, hidden and visible. Visible is driven by the trigger: :hover or :focus-within after --delay-tooltip (300 ms), and hidden again at once when the pointer or focus leaves. data-state on the trigger forces it here with no delay.

3 unreadOpen navigation
  • The tooltip uses the inverse surface: near-black on the light theme, near-white on the dark theme, so it always stands apart from the page.
  • WCAG 1.4.13: the tooltip is hoverable (the pointer can move onto it, across a bridge over the 8px gap, without it closing), dismissible (Escape) and persistent (it stays until hover or focus leaves).

Responsive behaviour

  • Tooltips are pointer and keyboard affordances. On touch screens (hover: none) they do not appear on tap; the accessible name still describes the control, and the icon must be recognisable without it.
  • Labels never wrap; keep them short enough to fit inside a 360px viewport when the trigger is near the edge.
  • Triggers in the top bar and other edge-hugging toolbars use .has-tooltip--below.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-inverse, --color-fg-inverse, --radius-sm, --shadow-2, --z-tooltip and the timing tokens --delay-tooltip and --duration-fast. A theme only needs to keep the inverse pair at 4.5:1; the arrow is drawn with the same inverse token.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tooltip: role="tooltip" with an id, referenced from the trigger by aria-describedby. The trigger keeps its own aria-label (“Notifications”); the tooltip adds the description (“3 unread”). When the tooltip text is the name itself (“Open navigation”), the duplicate description is harmless but may be dropped.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the trigger shows the tooltip after the delay.
EscHides the tooltip while focus stays on the trigger (a few lines of script toggle data-tooltip-hidden; CSS alone keeps it visible while focused).
Shift+Tab / pointer outHides it immediately.

Focus

The tooltip never takes focus and contains nothing focusable. The trigger shows the shared focus ring as well as the tooltip.

Labelling

Never rely on the tooltip for the accessible name: the trigger has aria-label even when a tooltip shows the same words.

Motion

Fades over --duration-fast after the 300 ms delay; reduced motion keeps the delay but removes the fade.

Touch

No tooltip on touch. Icons used without a tooltip on phones must be ones people know (bell, menu, close), or the control needs a visible label.

Content

  • One to four words, sentence case, no full stop: “Open navigation”, “Move hero down”, “3 unread”.
  • Say what the control does or what state it reports; do not repeat the icon's shape (“Bell”).
  • Add a shortcut only if it works on that page.
