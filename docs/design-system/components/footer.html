Footer

The footer closes guest-facing pages with a quiet “Sent with Invitely”. Organiser pages have no footer; the sidebar already holds everything they need.

Overview

The public event page, the RSVP form, the invalid-invitation page and the mocks layered on them (29 files) end with <footer class="footer">Sent with Invitely</footer> after <main>. Sign-in and the organiser app have none.

  • Simple: one line, centred. The default for guest pages.
  • With links: sign-off at the start, links at the end. For help, privacy and reporting an invitation once those pages exist.
  • Never put event actions (RSVP, add to calendar) in the footer; guests who need them should not have to scroll past the event.

Anatomy

  1. Sign-off--text-body-sm, --color-fg-muted.
  2. LinksInherit the muted colour, underlined, darken on hover; 16px apart.

Responsive behaviour

  • Padding is 32px vertically and --layout-margin horizontally, so the footer's text lines up with page content at every breakpoint.
  • With links: sign-off and links share one row (space-between) and wrap to two rows when they do not fit; at 360px they always wrap.
  • The footer is in normal flow after <main>; it is never fixed, so it cannot cover the toast region or a focused field.

Theming

Muted text on whatever the page canvas is, with a --color-border-default top rule. No background of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A top-level <footer> outside <main> is the contentinfo landmark. Links are a list so their count is announced.

Content

  • Link labels say where they go: “Help”, “Privacy”, “Report this invitation”.
