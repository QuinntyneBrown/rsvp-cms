Overview

Invitely commits to WCAG 2.2 Level AA for both the organiser app and the guest RSVP pages. The system is accessible by construction: tokens guarantee contrast, components bring their own keyboard model and focus ring, and every component page documents its role, keyboard map, focus, labelling, contrast, motion and touch. Guests include grandparents on old phones and people using screen readers to RSVP for a party; an invitation they cannot answer is a broken invitation.

The binding requirements are L2-084 (touch targets), L2-085 (contrast in both themes), L2-086 (keyboard and focus) and L2-087 (forms and errors) in docs/specs/L2.md; the Spec column below shows which rule satisfies which. Acceptance tests in tests/acceptance/ trace to those ids.

  • Automated: check_contrast.py (every token pair, both themes) and check_design_system.py (labels, names, alt text, landmarks, headings, links).
  • Manual: keyboard, screen reader, zoom, reduced motion and forced colours, as listed under Testing.

Target sizes

Two target tokens and three control heights. Every target is at least --target-min. On touch screens ((pointer: coarse)) every button, link and control reaches --target-comfortable, by padding its hit area where the drawing is smaller (L2-084).

Dashed boxes are the two target tokens at true size; the controls beside them are real.
TokenValue nowUse for
--target-minWCAG 2.5.8 minimum for any target: close buttons in alerts and toasts, chip remove buttons, choice rows, nav-group summaries.
--target-comfortableThe hit area of every button, link and control under (pointer: coarse) (L2-084); the drawn size of nav links, tabs and accordion summaries.

Control heights

Buttons, inputs and selects share three heights so a row of controls lines up.
TokenValue nowUse for
--control-height-smSmall buttons in table rows and toolbars, chips, pagination, menu items, small fields.

--control-height-lgLarge buttons on auth and public pages, large fields.

Focus

One focus indicator everywhere: a 2px outline in --color-focus-ring offset by 2px, shown on :focus-visible so pointer clicks do not leave rings behind. Inputs draw it at 0 offset with a matching border so it hugs the field; tabs and accordion summaries draw it inset so a scrolling container does not clip it.

  • Focus moves into a dialog on open (the first field, or the safe action in a destructive dialog), Tab cycles inside it, Esc closes it unless a request is in progress, and focus returns to the trigger (L2-086).
  • After an error summary appears on submit, focus moves to the summary.
  • After a busy button finishes, focus stays on it; do not add disabled while busy.
  • In forced-colours mode the ring becomes the system Highlight colour.

Component contract

Every component page's Accessibility section answers the same seven questions:

  1. Role and pattern: the element or WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices pattern, linked.
  2. Keyboard: a table of keys and effects.
  3. Focus: where focus starts, where it goes on close, how the ring looks.
  4. Labelling: what must be labelled, how, and what is announced.
  5. Contrast: the token pairs involved, with live ratios in both themes.
  6. Motion: what animates and what reduced motion does.
  7. Touch: target sizes and hover alternatives.

A new component is not done until its page answers all seven and its pairs are in contrast-pairs.json.
